What Nova does

Four jobs, done quietly.

  • Answers with receipts

    Every answer links the doc, thread or ticket it came from, so you can check in one click.

  • Meeting memory

    Nova listens to the calls you invite it to and keeps decisions, owners and dates — not transcripts nobody reads.

  • Drafts in your voice

    Replies, briefs and release notes that sound like your team, learned from what you've already written.

  • Hands off the busywork

    Files the ticket, books the follow-up, updates the doc. You approve; Nova does the clicking.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Your workspace is used only to answer your team's questions.

Which tools does Nova connect to?

Docs, chat, email, calendars and issue trackers — the usual suspects, through standard APIs.

Can Nova take actions on its own?

Only with your approval. Every action is shown first and logged.

What happens when the trial ends?

You drop to Spark automatically. Nothing is deleted, nothing is charged.

Is this a real product?

No — Nova is a fictional brand made for a design and engineering portfolio.
